# 2026-09-07 — the agent asks to share

The agent has no door that opens a brain to anyone. Its `share` tool writes
an ask: these records or types of the person's, to these colleagues, groups
or everyone, at this level, for this reason. The ask waits on the brain's
pages until the person accepts or declines it. Accepting makes the shares in
the person's name; either answer removes the ask. The log keeps the ask and
the shares it became.

## Why asking, not sharing

Sharing is the one act in the brain that reaches past its owner. Two
founders working through one brain need their agents to put things in
front of each other constantly, and a person still has to be the one who
decides what leaves their brain. An ask gives the agent the whole
mechanism and the person the whole decision, and the reason the agent gives
is what the person reads before deciding. Tanishk: "A share tool that only
asks: it writes a share request, batches of items to batches of people at
a level with a reason, and nothing is shared until the person accepts it in
the interface."

## The shape

One ask is a batch: many items to many parties, one level, one reason, and
it is taken whole. Parties are named as a person would name them, by a
colleague's email or a group's name, or "everyone", and resolved when the
ask is written, so an ask never names nobody. A record may be asked for by
whoever may share it, its owner or someone holding it at owner level; a type
only by whoever defined it. A colleague with less cannot ask on the owner's
behalf. Everyone can only be asked for at view, as with a share. The asks show at the top of the
brain's front page as sentences with Share and Not now.

## What it does not do

No expiry, no partial acceptance, no ask from a colleague for access to
something they cannot see, no notification beyond the page itself. Each is
a guess about how two people will use it; a week of use decides.
