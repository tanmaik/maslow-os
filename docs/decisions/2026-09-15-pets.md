# 2026-09-15 — the agent is pets (reversed the same morning)

**Reversed 2026-09-15, morning.** Tanmai, on seeing them: "i just dont get
how this works? … can we get rid of the pet style entirely and just bring
back the normal thing we had with the normal agent window." The pets are
gone; the Agent window is the one shape. What the night built underneath
stays: several conversations open at once in the door, a warm one for the
next, sleep after three minutes, the wakeup and monitor tools, the phone
shell, the 8 GB floor. The rest of this file is the decision as it was
made, kept for why it was tried.

The Agent window is no longer the way to the agent. Each conversation is
a pet: a small mark at the desktop's corner, one of several, that opens into
a hand-sized card and closes back into the mark. Behind it is the same
Claude Code, one process per open conversation, held by the door.

## Why

Tanmai, 2026-09-14: "instead of this whole agent app can we make a little
buddy that floats around the interface and doesn't take too much space
anywhere and just does little tasks… each new chat is a pet… show the
ones that are running… click into it… small." A full-window chat made the
agent a place to go; the work is on the desktop, and the agent should be
beside it. The same night he decided to keep building our own agent
interface rather than hand people plain Claude Code, on the condition
that it stays Claude Code behind the scenes and nothing of Claude Code's
is lost: steer and queue, wakeups, monitors, subagents, the seeded MCPs.

## What a pet is

- **A mark.** A letter of its name in a circle, in a cluster at the
  bottom right that is dragged anywhere and kept there on the device.
  Running turns a light around it; answered and unseen carries a dot and
  floats the first words for nine seconds; asleep is dim.
- **A card.** A click opens the thread and a plain pill over the desktop,
  the size of a hand. The plus opens a new one at once; the door keeps a
  warm session so the first word is not waited on.
- **Talked to.** Hold Option, or long-press the plus on a phone: what is
  heard is written as it is heard and sent when the key lifts, into the
  open pet or a new one.
- **Several at once.** The door holds a map of conversations, each with
  its own Claude Code process. Quiet for three minutes, one is closed and
  its process freed; it opens again whole when wanted. The open set is on
  the disk and reopened when the door starts.
- **Two tools of the door's own.** `schedule_wakeup` and `monitor`, a
  small MCP server started beside each conversation under a token of its
  own; a wakeup is kept on `/data` and fires into the running turn or as
  a turn of its own; a monitor runs a command as the person and hands the
  conversation its lines a second at a time. No cron, no workflow.

## What is not

- No full-page agent: the Agent window stays, as the same conversations
  at full size, and nothing more is built on it.
- No scheduling beyond a wakeup; no orchestration beyond Claude Code's
  own subagents.
- AskUserQuestion is not yet answered from the card; it waits on an
  adapter patch.

## Cost

A pet is a Claude Code process, and a 2 GB machine could not hold several
(see the memory lift in the sweep). The floor is 4 shared CPUs and 8 GB,
always on. Tanmai chose always on knowing the price: "okay fine lets do
the always on".
