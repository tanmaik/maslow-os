# 2026-09-09 — the room

Home is the room: what asks something of you. Today that is the agent's
asks to share, as the sentences the brain shows, with Share and Not now.
Later it is the pages teammates' agents put in front of you, live from
their computers, which is a decision of its own. Beside them, who is in
the org, by name. The canvas zooms. When nothing asks anything of you, the
room says so in one line.

## Why not what is shared with you

The first cut put every record a colleague shared with you by name or
with a group of yours on the canvas, and counted it as waiting on you.
Tanishk stopped it: "we're both going to have a lot of information about
how our meetings with customers or investors went. just because I or my
group was shared something from tanmai doesn't mean it's something that I
have to act on ... something about this seems too coupled." A shared
record is knowledge. It goes into your brain, where it is listed beside
your own types under whose it is, and it asks nothing. Sharing with
Everyone is the same door left wider. So the room holds only what was
built to ask: an ask from the agent, a page with a button on it. "Waiting
on you" is exactly that count, and it falls when the ask is answered or
the page is taken back; nothing tracks whether you opened anything.

## The shape

The page reads the caller's open asks through the brain package and draws
them; the count on the pill is the same read. Nothing is stored for the
room: no positions, no list of what is in it. Who is in the org comes from
the catalog.

## What it does not do

No pages from anyone's computer yet: a share that points at a port, and a
ticket that lets a colleague's browser reach it, are their own decision.
No cards for shared records, by name or otherwise. No slot for a goal and
no line for a prompt to your computer: those are records or pages an agent
writes and shares. No dot for whose computer is on.
