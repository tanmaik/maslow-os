# 2026-09-15 — arrival

A person's first desk meets them with one card, three questions asked one
at a time while their computer is made behind it. Nothing is a tour and
nothing is a form: each answer is written into something that already
exists, and each is a Settings pane later.

## Why

The heartbeat and the desk gave the agent a way to work on its own; the
person had no way to say how much of that they wanted except by editing
a file on their computer. And Maslow is sold as clarity for someone
managing many long-term things at once; the first minute should be the
first moment of it. Tanishk, 2026-09-15: "Im scared itll take so long and
people won't like it. we need some way to keep full functionality but
reduce perceived latency."

## What it is

- **The desk first.** Sign-in lands on the desk, dock and all, with the
  card in the middle and the computer being made behind it; the wait is
  the Computer pane's bar, seen through the card's own words. Nothing
  waits on the machine but the machine's own windows.
- **How much your agent does on its own.** Arrange my desk, once a day;
  tell me in the bar, every half hour; nothing. The answer sets the
  heartbeat's cadence, which is a setting under Computer from then on,
  and for the first two writes a paragraph, in words the person could
  have written, into a section of their own `CLAUDE.md` on their
  computer, once the computer is ready. That file is theirs to edit, in
  the Terminal or Files, and the card says so; a Settings control that
  rewrites the paragraph is a follow-up. The brief stays mechanisms.
- **Which apps it may read.** The few most connected, each a Connect
  that opens the app's sign-in in a new tab, as the Connected apps pane
  would. Skip is a button.
- **What you are trying to get to.** The next year or two, several
  things, half-formed is fine. This is the heart of the card and the
  product's pitch in ninety seconds: a person who has never written
  their long-term goals in one place does it here. The words are not
  turned into records of a type we chose, since the brain has no goal
  type and should not get one from us; they are the reason the first run
  is given, "the person just arrived and said what they are working
  toward: …", and the agent writes them into the brain in its own
  vocabulary and puts its first thing in front of the person.
- **Delivered when the computer is ready.** The answer is kept on the
  person's row until their computer answers its door, then delivered
  once: the paragraph into their file, the first run woken. Answered
  after the computer is ready, it is delivered at once.
- **Once.** Skipped or answered, the card never comes back. Skipped
  changes nothing, so a person who already had a cadence keeps it;
  "nothing on its own" chosen outright sets the cadence off, and does
  not ask what they are working toward, since no run would be told.
  Everything it set is under Settings → Computer and Connected apps.
- **Delivered once.** The first run is asked for under a key the
  machine answers once, however often it is asked, so a delivery cut off
  after the machine heard finishes the rest on its next try without a
  second run, and one cut off before it heard tries again.

## What it is not

- Not a wizard. Three rows on a card that leaves, not pages.
- Not a place the product tells the agent what matters. The person's
  words go to the agent; our words describe what each surface is for.
- Not pre-warmed machines to hide the two minutes: a bar is honest and
  costs nothing.
